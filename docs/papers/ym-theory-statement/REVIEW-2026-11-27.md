# The Lowest Note: accuracy and narrative review

27 November 2026. Reviewed starting from `1904d315` on `feat/helike-carrier-geometry-probe`.

## Purpose and overall reading

This is a description of a proposed mechanism, not a proof submission. The review asks whether the geometric
objects, relationships, numbers and labels describe that mechanism accurately. An unproved conjecture is not an
editorial defect. The central proposal and the five-part narrative remain intact.

The strongest organizing idea is to follow the geometry of the candidate excitation, not just its eigenvalue:
identify a shape in the small room, follow its competition with another shape, describe what finer modes do to it,
and ask what persists as the room grows. This connects the finite calculations to a physical picture without
making the finite calculations stand in for the whole picture.

The central distinction is now clearer: the carrier may change while a positive gap persists. Conversely, following
a recognizable carrier is not identical to following the lowest energy over all physical states. That distinction
helps explain the theory; it is not a demand to stop describing it until a proof exists.

## Principal corrections

### 1. The path is not the whole landscape

The midpoint of the straight Maurer-Cartan line maximizes the potential restricted to that line. On a Berger
sphere it is not generally a critical point of the full potential. At `t=3, g=1`, writing the three diagonal
coordinates as `x,y,z`, the gradient at the midpoint is

\[
  \nabla V=(\sqrt3/6,-1/12,-1/12).
\]

Its dot product with the line tangent `(1/sqrt(3),1,1)` vanishes, but the gradient does not. At `t=1` it does vanish.
Section 4 therefore says **path maximum**, reserving the full sphaleron identification for the round case.

Likewise, the negative eigendirection of the Chern-Simons Hessian at the MC well is not the straight-line tangent
when `t != 1`. Section 5 describes it as a candidate departure direction of a curved transition path. This is a
sharper geometric picture, not a rejection of the proposed topological mechanism.

### 2. Three objects had been sharing the name "competitor"

- The local charge-0 harmonic mode at the MC well has frequency `(sqrt(t+8)-sqrt(t))/2`.
- In the full undressed two-well reduction, at leading harmonic order and `t>1`, the other well's ground state is
  the lowest charge-0 excitation above the zero-field well. Its offset equals that frequency, but it is a different state.
- In the full gauge theory, zero field and the MC field are gauge copies. The physical vacuum-chart charge-0 and
  charge-2 two-quantum channels tie at leading order. Their finite-coupling ordering is the proposed physical H8.

The text now distinguishes these meanings rather than changing the theory's proposed connection between them.
The supporting H8 document leaves the registered finite couplings conjectural and gives an asymptotic proof sketch;
the earlier register's blanket statement that H8 was proved for the reduced operator was too strong.

The `0.3624` margin is harmonic and belongs to the two-well picture. The derived `5.18` candidate budget is not a
margin for the physically tied pair. The missing kinetic contribution is part of the complete order-`g^2`
splitting, not an established single cause of its sign.

### 3. Exact crossing describes what happens if levels meet

Different conserved charges prevent the two channels from mixing. This rules out an avoided crossing between them,
but does not force a meeting or identify every competing channel. The harmonic switch formula remains unchanged.
The registered stronger-coupling switches remain conjectures. The value `g^2 ~= 0.0075` is a first-order crossover
estimate, not a proved interval in which the finite-coupling ordering is known.

Ordered eigenvalues can be continuous through a crossing while their individually energy-ordered eigenvectors or
projections change abruptly. The text now assigns continuous projections to isolated spectral clusters.

### 4. Fine modes do more than run the coupling

The matched logarithm describes ultraviolet running. Finite potential and kinetic contributions still move the
levels. "They dress the note without erasing it" remains the proposed mechanism; "they only run the coupling"
would contradict the finite dressing computed in the same note.

The saved seven-point comparison in `research/gigi-m3-20261127/result.json` has maximum relative discrepancy
`0.0007943073413110291`. Its central five points are within `0.0000665648383482`. The paper now reports bounds
`7.95e-4` and `6.66e-5`, not `1e-4` across all seven. These are observed discrepancies, not statistical intervals.

The potential-only separation remains `0.0165495762844 -> 0.0190663957785` at `g^2=0.002`; the numerical result was
not changed. Four-digit spectral-truncation agreement is distinguished from the wider regulator-window variation.
The loop relabelling receipt gives maximum change `1.2212453270876722e-15`, so its displayed bound is `1.23e-15`.

### 5. The scale and the limit need the right descriptive units

`Lambda` is an energy scale; `1/Lambda` is its associated length in natural units. At finite radius the dimensionless
ratio may depend on `R Lambda`. A section positive at every finite radius can approach zero at infinity, so the
intended persistent note is described by a uniform lower bound, not merely the phrase "nowhere vanishing".

The theory's all-radius bound remains its chosen route. It is stronger than the large-radius liminf alone needed
for the limit step. No new large-scale numerical run was performed or requested by this edit.

## Other precision edits

- Constant gauge rotations preserve the entire homogeneous ansatz; nonconstant gauge transformations can still
  identify particular homogeneous fields. The signed-polar measure is local, with global multiplicities to handle.
- Helike's exact eigenvector formula is separated from the floor theorem's stated field domain. The sourced controls
  lie outside that floor domain; the exact doublet transfers without importing an unsupported floor assertion.
- Peter-Weyl gives the field and Hessian decomposition, not independent sectors of the interacting Hamiltonian.
  The background-coordinate map is called `M=2gq`, not confused with curvature `F`.
- The Wilson plaquette cost is half a **squared** unit-quaternion chord. Internal double-cover representations are
  not spacetime spin, and do not alone establish confinement.
- Complete compatible loop data can reconstruct a smooth connection under the reconstruction theorem's hypotheses;
  a finite collection of observed loops is not that complete data. Gauge-invariant local quantities also exist.
- A mass gap constrains states orthogonal to the vacuum, not the mean energy of arbitrary vacuum superpositions.
- The finite-lattice Hamiltonian perturbation statement is not silently made uniform in volume using a theorem
  about a different Euclidean object.
- Positive four-dimensional flow smears time as well as space. Its bounded classical readings are distinguished
  from physical time-slice operators and local fields in the continuum description.
- The spectral-limit lemma now explicitly uses positive autocorrelation measures, dense vacuum-orthogonal states
  and uniform operator bounds. These spell out what its existing argument uses.
- The Euclidean anomaly sign, component normalization, six-plaquette convention and integrated insertion are
  explicit. The canonical `O` is not silently interchanged with an unspecified normalization of `tr F^2`.
- A nonzero connected third moment does not exclude Gaussian *constituents*: for a standard Gaussian `X`,
  `Y=X^2-1` has third cumulant `8`. The stated tests distinguish particular free-field comparisons, not every
  possible full observable algebra.
- The all-group box is split into cited lattice results and the electric/embedded-mode statements. An embedded
  exact doublet is not automatically the floor of additional colour blocks.

The register is synchronized with these scopes and logs the corrections. Historical proof drafts and earlier
review notes are retained; they are not silently rewritten as though they had always made these distinctions.

## Sources checked for the corrections

- [Jaffe and Witten's official Clay description](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf),
  particularly printed pp. 5-6: the intended nontrivial theory, local fields, short-distance behavior and a finite
  nonempty excitation threshold. This clarified the existing target, not an additional requirement invented here.
- [Luscher and Weisz, 1101.0963](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.0963): the positive-flow finiteness statement is
  perturbative to all loop orders after ordinary four-dimensional renormalization.
- [Suzuki, 1304.0533](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.0533): the local conserved stress tensor is connected to flowed
  products by small-flow-time matching, rather than by simply identifying a positive-flow product with it.
- Repository register, H2/H8 notes, working report and saved numerical receipts supplied the internal scope checks.

This was not a fresh proof audit of every cited theorem or a rerun of the research programme. It does not certify
H1-H8, establish a mass gap, or adjudicate priority. It reviews the accuracy of the description.
