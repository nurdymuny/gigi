\section{Statement status and coverage}\label{app:register}

\subsection*{How to read the statements}

This is a narrative theory, not a proof, told as one story from the problem (\S1)
to what the gap is (\S10). Every important statement, proved or not, has a number and a label giving its
standing: \textcolor{teal}{\textbf{PROVED}} (derived here) and \textcolor{teal}{\textbf{PROVED (CITED)}} (cited from a separate work, including my own),
\textcolor{sky}{\textbf{COMPUTED}} (numerically, not proved), \textcolor{gold}{\textbf{CONDITIONAL}} (proved from a
named hypothesis), \textcolor{plum}{\textbf{CONJECTURE}}, and \textcolor{muted}{\textbf{OPEN}}. Each statement's source entry names the lines
of my register of statements~\cite{ClosedSet} it covers, and no statement is stronger than its line. All 29 lines (29 is
merged into 23) appear in Table~\ref{tab:register}. The finer evidence, mine and
others', is in the companion blog post and the repository~\cite{HelikeReport}.

\subsection*{The five points and their standing}

(1)~\emph{The gap has a name}: Helike is proved and cited
(Statement~\ref{b:helike}), crossings between the two charges are exact if they occur (Statement~\ref{b:cross}), and that the carrier changes only at
the switches is a conjecture (Statement~\ref{b:switch}). (2)~\emph{The landscape gives the carriers their costs}: topology
organizes the routes, the reduced geometry gives the harmonic ordering (Statement~\ref{b:susy}), the reduced model keeps
the scalar below Helike through second order (Statement~\ref{b:origin}), and at small coupling H8 holds in the reduced
model, conditionally (Statement~\ref{b:smallg}). The full quantum dynamics determines the physical carrier; its
identification is H8 (Statement~\ref{b:h8}, open). (3)~\emph{Tensor and scalar trade
places at a calculable point}: $s_0^*$ is proved at harmonic order (Statement~\ref{b:switch0}); the registered $s^*$ are a
conjecture (Statement~\ref{b:switch}). (4)~\emph{The fine grain cannot erase the note}: $b_0$ is computed (Statement~\ref{b:b0}),
H2 is a conjecture (Statement~\ref{b:m3}), and the kinetic contribution is open (Statement~\ref{b:kinetic}). (5)~\emph{The infinite world is
the last room}: the scaling reduction and the spectral lemma are proved (Statements~\ref{b:scaling}, \ref{b:lemma}); H3 and
H4$'$ are conjectures (Statements~\ref{b:h3}, \ref{b:h4}).
