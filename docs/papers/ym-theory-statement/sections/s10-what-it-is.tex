\section*{10. What the mass gap is}

\textbf{(a) The theory, and where each of its points stands.} (1)~\emph{The gap has a name}: Helike is proved and cited
(Box~\ref{b:helike}), crossings are exact (Box~\ref{b:cross}, proved), and that the carrier changes only at
the switches is a conjecture (Box~\ref{b:switch}). (2)~\emph{Topology decides who is lightest}: proved in the reduced model
(Box~\ref{b:susy}); in the full theory it waits on H8 (Box~\ref{b:h8}, open). (3)~\emph{Tensor and scalar trade
places at a calculable point}: $s_0^*$ is proved at harmonic order (Box~\ref{b:switch0}); the registered $s^*$ are a
conjecture (Box~\ref{b:switch}). (4)~\emph{The fine grain cannot erase the note}: $b_0$ is computed (Box~\ref{b:b0}),
H2 is a conjecture (Box~\ref{b:m3}), and one kinetic term is open (Box~\ref{b:kinetic}). (5)~\emph{The infinite world is
the last room}: the scaling reduction and the spectral lemma are proved (Boxes~\ref{b:scaling}, \ref{b:lemma}); H3 and
H4$'$ are conjectures (Boxes~\ref{b:h3}, \ref{b:h4}).

\begin{claim}{CONJECTURE}{The lowest note (the theory)}\label{b:theory}
The mass gap of quantum Yang--Mills theory is the energy of the cheapest topology-leaning excitation, carried by an
identified shape in every room of the sphere family, with exact switches of carrier; it survives the ultraviolet because
the fast modes only run the coupling, and the infrared because $\Delta(R)/\Lambda$ is a nowhere-vanishing section whose
$R\to\infty$ end is the $\mathbb R^4$ theory.
\evidence{Proved: lines 1--10, 13, 17, 19, 22, the lemma of 28 (2, 3, 10 cited); 21 cited, partial. Computed: 14, 15
(potential part). Conditional: 12, 26, 27, the deduction of 28, the existence of a switch. Conjectured: 18 and H1, H2, H3,
H4$'$, H6, H7. Open: H8 in the physical theory.}
\lines{11, 12, 17--20, 23, 28}
\end{claim}

\textbf{(b) What is known, and what would be new.} Simulations show a gap with the scalar lightest~\cite{MorningstarPeardon99};
perturbation theory shows asymptotic freedom~\cite{GrossWilczek73,Politzer73}; strong-coupling expansions prove a gap at
the wrong end of the coupling~\cite{OsterwalderSeiler78}. They have been three separate facts. My theory gives the reason
for each (leaning toward a change of topology costs energy; the cheapest lean is a scalar along the Chern--Simons path;
the fast modes do to the note only what they do to the coupling) and says they are one fact seen from three sides: the
lowest note of the folded space of field shapes, named in the small room and followed to infinite space. The finite-volume road
is L\"uscher's and van Baal's~\cite{Luscher83,KollerVanBaal88,vanBaal00}; the stretched sphere, the identified floor, the
named carrier, the sources, the exact switches and the inheritance along scale are mine.

\textbf{(c) How it can fail.} Each of these would break the theory at a named place. \emph{$S_C$ fails}: R2 finds a third
charge-0 level, and Box~\ref{b:order} falls. \emph{The kinetic term reverses the order}: Box~\ref{b:kinetic} outweighs
Box~\ref{b:widen}; then H8 fails and point 2 holds for the reduced model only. \emph{No switch where predicted}: numerics find no exchange near $s_0^*$ and $s^*$. \emph{The carrier
dissolves without a crossing}: then the gap is made somewhere, not inherited. \emph{$\delta(x)$ grows sublinearly}: every
room is gapped but $\Delta/\Lambda\to0$, the failure H3 exists to exclude. \emph{The $c_0$ tuning fails}: the last room is
not $\mathbb R^4$. \emph{The fingerprint is missing}: $|x|^8\langle\Theta\Theta\rangle$ does not fall like $1/\ln^2$, or
$T_O=0$ and the generalized-free-field loophole stays open.

\textbf{(d) Completeness.} Box~\ref{b:clay} is the whole Clay statement. Table~1 places every register
line~\cite{ClosedSet} with its status and boxes; Table~2 maps every clause of the problem~\cite{JaffeWitten,OS73,OS75} to
its lines and boxes. Nothing in either table is left without a box.

\textbf{(e) What this is.} A complete narrative theory of the Yang--Mills mass gap: complete, because every clause has a
line and every line a place in the story; unproven, because H3, H4$'$, H6 and H7 are conjectures, and H3 is the wall
itself; falsifiable, because each claim has a place where the geometry can tell me no. What I offer here is the shape of the answer: the gap is the lowest note of a
field that cannot change its topology for free, and it is the same note in every room.
