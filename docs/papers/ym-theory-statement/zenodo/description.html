This version replaces my earlier posting on this record, which claimed a proof of the Yang–Mills mass gap. That claim is withdrawn. The earlier version remains in the record's history, superseded. What this version offers is different in kind: a theory, not a proof. It is my complete account, as of 27 November 2026, of what I believe the mass gap is, with every statement labelled by its standing.

The theory in one sentence

The mass gap is the lowest excitation energy produced by topology, geometry and quantum dynamics together. Topology organizes the routes through the space of field shapes; the metric, the potential and the kinetic energy give those routes their costs; quantum dynamics sets the lowest note above the vacuum. In my picture that note is carried by an identified shape in every room of a family of stretched three-spheres, the carrier changes only at exact switches, the fine grain dresses the note without erasing it, and the gap stays positive in the field's own energy scale out to infinite space.

The five points

  1. The gap has a name. In a small room it is carried either by Helike, a twisting shape of charge 2 under the room's rotation symmetry, or by her competitor, a breathing shape of charge 0. Shapes of different charge cannot mix, so if their levels meet the crossing is exact.
  2. Topology organizes who is lightest. With the sources off, the reduced potential is exactly the squared slope of the Chern–Simons function: V = ½|∇W|2 with W = CS/(2√t g2) on homogeneous fields, so H = ½Σ ai*ai + 3S/2. At the winding-one vacuum the competitor is the one downhill direction of W, the field's first step toward the other winding vacuum, and it is the softest physical mode there for every stretch t. This suggests a mechanism for the scalar glueball being lightest. In the reduced model it now holds one order further: at the zero-field well Helike's pair is unshifted to every order, while the scalar level drops by 2κ(1+1/t)g2 (16g2/3 for SU(2) at t = 3). At small enough coupling, H8 and the existence of a switch follow by deduction from two named inputs. In the physical theory the ordering still waits on the full dressed O(g2) splitting.
  3. Tensor-like and scalar-like channels trade places at a calculable point. At harmonic order the switch sits at s0*(t) = (t+2)(√(t+8) − √t)/(4√t), which is 1.1436 at t = 3. The registered switches at stronger coupling (1.1674, 1.1809, 1.1931 at g2 = 0.03, 0.05, 0.07) rise from it.
  4. The fine grain dresses the note. On the Berger sphere, the logarithmic divergence of the fast modes' zero-point energy has exactly the asymptotically free coefficient b0 = 22/3 (to within 6.7×10−5 at the central points). The finite potential dressing widens Helike's lead at the controls (0.0165 → 0.0191 at g2 = 0.002). A kinetic correction of the same order is not computed; in a linear estimate at the controls it would have to exceed a definite threshold (κ > 7.33 at g2 = 0.002) to reverse the order, with no remainder bound yet.
  5. The infinite world is the last room. Space is never built as a box and argued away: infinite space is defined as the end of the family of spheres, and the gap is a section over that family which, I conjecture, never touches zero in units of the theory's scale Λ.

What is proved, computed and conjectured

Every statement in the document carries one of six labels: proved (derived there), proved (cited), computed (numerical, not proved), conditional (proved from a named hypothesis), conjecture, and open. An appendix maps every line of my register of statements, and every clause of the official Clay problem description, to the statements that address it. In brief:

  • Proved or cited: gauge invariance and the lattice measure on connections modulo gauge; reflection positivity (Osterwalder–Seiler); the electric strong-coupling gap 3g2/(2a), the Casimir of one turn; the exact two-dimensional area law with Casimir scaling; the slow space ℝ9/SO(3); the Chern–Simons line, its vacua and the background; the superpotential identity; discreteness of the reduced spectrum (Theorem A), and its extension to every compact simple group through a cubic–quartic bound; a strict vacuum floor; the exact inter-well action d = 1/(2√t), so the Chern–Simons (Bogomolny) bound is attained; a sharp horizontal charge barrier; Helike as an exact eigenvector and floor (By Enough, Floor); the harmonic switch and the second-order splitting at the origin; the source bridge s → 1+; the vanishing of the free Wick triangle, which refuted an earlier prediction of mine; and the spectral lemma by which a uniform gap survives the limit.
  • Computed: the b0 coefficient of the fast dressing; the free four-point coefficient 676 dim(g)/(27π8); the finite potential dressing at the background; the kinetic threshold of the linear estimate; the GIGI bundle constructions that reproduce the exact answers.
  • Conditional or conjectural: SC and the ordering at the controls; H8 and the switch at small coupling (conditional on a cited semiclassical limit), and at the registered couplings (open); uniform dressing at small radius (M3); the gap uniform in the room's size (M2, the infrared wall); the continuum limit on the sphere family with full Euclidean symmetry; local fields and short-distance agreement with perturbation theory; non-triviality; every compact simple gauge group.

The construction is complete in the sense that every step the Clay problem requires is named and placed. It is not validated as a whole, and the mass gap is not proved.

What would be new, if it is true

Simulations already show a gap with the scalar glueball lightest, and perturbation theory establishes asymptotic freedom. What this theory adds is a proposed reason connecting the three: the lowest note of the folded space of field shapes, named in the small room and followed to infinite space.
