# Seeing, feeling, imagining: how I look for her (21 November 2026)

For thousands of years people innovated because they **saw** something while they **felt** it and **imagined** it.
I hold that as a trichotomy, and none of the three may rule the other two:

- **Imagination** proposes. Left alone, it builds castles.
- **Feeling** points. Left alone, it believes whatever it wants.
- **Seeing** is the check. The physical world is the real world, and something can be seen emerging from it. But to
  see, you have to **look**.

## How this shaped the work on the twist

- **I felt it first:** the twist is the most beautiful thing I have seen in mathematics; the direction is what
  governs her.
- **I imagined forms for that feeling, and each became a pre-registered claim:**
  - the skeleton;
  - the field strength aligned with her spin;
  - aligned *and* singled out.
- **Then I looked,** with tests written down before the data. Each imagined form failed, and each failure showed me
  more of where she actually is:
  - Test 2: three families where she is never the floor;
  - Test 4: aligned backgrounds where she wins only the uniform sector;
  - Test 5: singled-out backgrounds, the same.

Feeling was not wrong to point. Imagination was not wrong to propose. Looking decided, and looking is what turned a
feeling into a map.

## What looking means in practice

- **Write the prediction before the data.** Then a result can surprise me, and a surprise is information.
- **Look at her literally:** her shape on the sphere, and the region of fields where she is the floor, drawn as
  pictures, not only as numbers.
- **Keep the failures in the record.** They are the edges of the map.
- **Let what is proved stay proved, let what failed stay failed, and let a lead stay a lead** until it is tested.

The next step is to map the whole circle-symmetric family (x, y, t) and draw it. In that family the Berger field's
long-circle strength is x = (2 − t)/√t, which turns negative past t = 2, exactly where its lift coupling vanishes.
Every other background tested so far has x > 0. Whether that sign is her boundary is something to see, not to
assume.
