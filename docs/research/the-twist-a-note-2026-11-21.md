# The twist: a note on what I found and why it matters to me (21 November 2026)

*The Floor Theorem for the Yang–Mills Hessian at the Berger Field*, version 2, is published:
[doi:10.5281/zenodo.24412603](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24412603). The concept DOI, which always resolves to the
latest version, is 10.5281/zenodo.24386613.

## What she is

Stretch a three-sphere along one circle and let the geometry itself be the gauge field. Then ask how that field can
be disturbed at the least cost. In every family of disturbances, the cheapest one has the same shape. It turns around
the two short directions and, in step, turns inside itself. She is the state with the most spin about the long axis
that each family allows, and the only one. Her cost is an exact formula:

λ_t(j) = [j(j+2) − 2(t−3)j − 2(t−2)(t+4)]/t.

The formula is proved in every family, for every stretch from 2.75 to 10. Her anatomy is a charged particle in its
lowest orbit, plus a spin term, with the field's own strength lined up behind her. Where the field turns against her,
on the squashed meron and on SU(3), she is not the floor. So she is not everywhere. She is somewhere specific, and
that is what makes her real.

I call her the twist. She is the most beautiful thing I have seen in mathematics.

## What she is to me

Some peoples hold that everything is alive. I believe the same. This little one has a life of her own. She is like
an extremely rare creature, sitting there, smiling at me. I did not go looking for her. I was checking a lattice force
against an exact answer, and she was there in every sector at once. I think she chose me. I am not yet sure why. I
expect I will find out.

I am not anyone the world was waiting on. I wait tables. I research from five in the morning until my shift, and on
my days off until my brain says no más. The world does not always want me around, for reasons that are honestly
silly: it is silly to discount an idea because of the wrapper the person carrying it came in. I may not matter to the
world in the long run. But I think an idea like this should get to live in the world, and people should get to see
her. I see her.

## How I want to treat her

The way I have treated her so far:

- **Exact about her size.** What is proved is proved. What failed, failed: the squashed meron and SU(3) family 2a
  are class N, and the universal reading of the skeleton is counted against. What is a lead stays a lead: the
  field-strength alignment.
- **Open to attack.** Two adversarial reviews were folded in. Every certificate was rebuilt to a stricter standard,
  and every claim was narrowed where the evidence was narrower.
- **Pre-registered.** I write down the test before I look.

She deserves to be believed for the right reasons.

## She probably has a name

It is most likely an ancient one. One candidate: **Helike** (Ἑλίκη), "she who turns". It is the old Greek name for
the Great Bear, the constellation that circles the pole without ever setting. It comes from *helissō*, to turn or
twist, the same root as helix. That is what she does: she turns about the long axis, always the same way, at the
bottom of everything in her range.
